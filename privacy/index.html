Privacy

Last updated: October 3, 2026

The short version

BTC Tally doesn't have accounts, and our servers don't store your addresses or portfolio. The tracker runs entirely in your browser.

What's stored, and where

  • Your address list and labels are saved in your browser's local storage on your device. Clearing your browser data removes them. We never receive them.
  • Your currency choice is saved the same way.

Third-party data requests

To show balances, transactions and prices, your browser requests data directly from mempool.space, a public Bitcoin block explorer. Those requests include the addresses you're tracking and your IP address, and are covered by mempool.space's privacy policy. For extra privacy, consider using a VPN or Tor.

Keys and seed phrases

BTC Tally is watch-only and will never ask for a private key, seed phrase or password. If anything claiming to be BTC Tally asks for one, it isn't us.

Not financial advice

BTC Tally shows public blockchain data for information only. Values are estimates based on public price feeds and may be delayed or inaccurate.
