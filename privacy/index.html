Privacy

Last updated: October 3, 2026

What we store

  • Account: when you sign in with GitHub or Google, we receive your email address and a user ID from that provider. We never see your password.
  • Public profile: your trader name, optional bio, signals, comments and votes. These are public.
  • Reports you send are visible only to moderators.

Account data is stored with our database provider, Supabase.

Third parties

  • The chart is provided by TradingView and loads from their servers.
  • Live prices load from the public Coinbase Exchange API.

Your choices

You can change your trader name and bio at any time. To delete your account and all its content, contact us. Signals that were already closed are part of the public record until the account is deleted.

Never share keys

BTC Tally will never ask for private keys, seed phrases or exchange API keys.
