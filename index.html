Frequently asked questions

How do I track my Bitcoin balance?

Open the tracker and paste a Bitcoin address from your wallet. BTC Tally reads its balance and transaction history from the public blockchain. Add as many addresses as you like and they're totaled together.

Do I need to create an account?

No. There's no sign-up and no login. Your address list is saved in your own browser, so it's there next time you visit on the same device.

Is it safe? Will you ever ask for my keys?

Never. BTC Tally is watch-only: it only needs public addresses, which can't be used to move funds. Never enter a private key or seed phrase into any website.

Can I see historical value?

Yes. Every transaction shows what it was worth on the day it happened and what it's worth now, and the chart shows your portfolio value over time.

Where does the data come from?

Balances and transactions come from the public Bitcoin blockchain via mempool.space, and prices from mempool.space's price feed. Your browser requests it directly.

Does it support xpub / whole-wallet tracking?

Not yet. For now, add individual addresses. Extended public key (xpub) support is planned.
